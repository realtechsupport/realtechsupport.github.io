Infrastructures of Excess, 2026 - 2027

spatial systems that produce, manage, or materially express intensification, surplus, waste, and unequal accumulation

Infrastructures of Excess examines the material landscapes of heavy industry, waste, artificial intelligence and luxury. Satellite imagery connects individual sites to wider patterns of extraction, accumulation and unequal access to land and resources.

An archiving machine traces the contours of human-produced excess across the surface of the Earth. Drawing on a global collection of satellite imagery from low Earth orbit, two dozen AI agents interpret landscapes of excess—from sites of heavy industry and factory farming to AI factories and luxury enclaves—for traces of the forces that brought them into being. Cast in the austere visual language of a preliminary report, the video invites viewers to consider the cost of comfort and to follow the agents as they struggle to infer, from open-pit mines or superyacht marinas, the human demands that gave those landscapes their formt.
