Infrastructures of Excess, 2026

satellite imagery and artificial intelligence across landscapes of excess

Infrastructures of Excess examines the material landscapes of heavy industry, waste, artificial intelligence and luxury. Satellite imagery connects individual sites to wider patterns of extraction, accumulation and unequal access to land and resources.

A moving-image sequence brings together a world map, satellite views and interpretations generated by multiple AI models. Images, site information and critical prompts guide these readings. The project considers both what these systems reveal about the landscapes and what their interpretations leave out.
